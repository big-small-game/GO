Mines Strategies & Tips

If you want to know the best strategies and tips for playing Mines, take a look at the smartest options below. While this game uses a random number generator (RNG) to determine the wins, you can still adjust your betting style for the best possible outcome.

Low Risk Strategy

A low-risk strategy is the best way to learn more about this game of chance. You can start with a low number of mines on the grid and small wagers to test the waters. From there, you can slowly increase your betting options, and the multiplier percentage increase could work in your favour.

High Risk Strategy

If you prefer to bet big, then you can opt for a high-risk betting strategy. Setting bigger bets using this strategy means that your game outcome can yield very impressive multipliers! But be warned, keep an eye on your budget to avoid draining it too quickly.

Martingale Strategy

The Martingale strategy is a system that works by doubling your bet after every loss you experience. The objective behind this strategy is that eventually, when a win lands, you’ll recoup your losses because of the size of your bet. However, it can also mean playing through your budget fairly quickly if you’re on a losing streak, so proceed with caution.
